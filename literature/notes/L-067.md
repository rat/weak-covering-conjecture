## The relevant content

Siegel defines, for odd prime `q`, the "numen" `chi_q: Z_2 -> Z_q` of the shortened `qx+1` map,
characterized as the unique function satisfying

    f(2z) = f(z)/2,    f(2z+1) = (3f(z)+1)/2      (for q = 3)

subject to a limit condition along the truncations `[z]_{2^n}`. His Correspondence Principle
states that a nonzero integer `x` is a periodic point of `T_q` if and only if `x = chi_q(z)` for
some `z` in `Z_2 \ {0,1,2,...}`.

Section 3.1 is what matters here. Siegel states flatly that `chi_3` is equivalent to Tao's
Syracuse random variables, that the Syracuse random variables are the projections mod `3^n` of
this single `3`-adic-valued random variable, and that Tao chose not to pursue that formalism. He
then writes the object Tao's hardest estimate controls as

    phi_3(t) = integral over Z_2 of exp(-2 pi i {t chi_3(z)}_3) dz,     t in dual of Z_3,

with `{.}_3` the `3`-adic fractional part and `dz` the real-valued Haar probability measure on
`Z_2`, restates Tao's Proposition 1.17 in that language as `|phi_3(t)| <= C (log_3 |t|_3)^{-A}`,
inverts it by discrete Fourier transform to recover `P(chi_3 = m mod 3^n)`, and derives the
functional equation

    phi_3(t) = (1/2) phi_3(t/2) + (1/2) exp(-2 pi i {t/2}_3) phi_3(3t/2)

by splitting the defining integral over `2 Z_2` and `2 Z_2 + 1`. He notes explicitly that this is
the same recursion Tao records as Lemma 1.12.

## Verdict for this project

**Corroboration, not a new tool.** H-003's round 23 established the object identity between
`R_{l-1,j} mod 3^l`, Tao's `Syrac(Z/3^n Z)`, and a common core sum `G`, and verified it by exact
set equality in `verify_syrac_fourier.py`. This paper says, in print and four years earlier, the
`3`-adic half of the same thing: the Syracuse random variables are one `3`-adic random variable
seen mod `3^n`. That is worth having as a citable statement rather than only as this project's own
computation.

What it does not supply is any bound. The paper's own target is periodic points; `phi_3` appears
in Section 3.1 as background, and every quantitative statement about it is quoted from Tao. The
`n^{-A}` versus `3^{-n}` gap identified in round 23's Finding 6 is untouched here. The rest of the
paper (the `(p,q)`-adic spectral theory programme) was already assessed at L-013 and found to have
no covering or predecessor-density content.

One small correction to the index's earlier picture: L-013's entry records "one purely
bibliographic mention of Wirsching, a reading-list citation". The same holds here, reference [69]
in this paper's bibliography, with no technical engagement.
