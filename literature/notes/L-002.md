# L-002: G. J. Wirsching, "On positive predecessor density in 3n+1 dynamics"

Published version: Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems 9(3), 771-787 (2003). The copy
read here is a "(preliminary version)" preprint hosted at
https://www.math.uni-bielefeld.de/baake/algdyn/posden.pdf (a math-department mirror, found via

## Central finding: this paper does NOT extend or use the Weak Covering Conjecture / R_{j,k}

This is the important correction to the assumption in this project's `literature/INDEX.md`
entry going in ("likely the paper most likely to contain any further numerical/theoretical
development of the covering conjecture beyond the 1998 book"). That assumption was wrong.
Grepped the full text for "covering", "R_{j,k}"-style notation, and "weak covering": no hits.
Every citation back to the 1998 book (L-001, cited here as [4]) is to Chapters II, III, and IV
(general Collatz-graph structure, "balls in constrained urns," a functional differential
equation), never to Chapter V (where Corollary/Notation 1.8's R_{j,k} and Conjecture 3.9's Weak
Covering Conjecture actually live). Wirsching's own follow-up work on predecessor density took
a **different technical route entirely**, not an extension of the covering-conjecture chapter
this project is built around.

## What the paper actually does

Same overarching goal as the 1998 book's Reduction Theorem 3.10 (uniform positive predecessor
density for the 3n+1 map), pursued via a different apparatus:

- **Elka functions**: `e_l(k,a) := |E_{l,k}(a)|`, counting paths of length `l+k` in the Collatz
  graph from some `b` back to `a` using exactly `l` steps of `T1` (the `(3n+1)/2` branch) and
  `k` steps of `T0` (the `/2` branch). Key property (cited from the 1998 book, Section II.4, not
  Chapter V): `a == b mod 3^l` implies `e_l(k,a) = e_l(k,b)` for all k, which lets Elka functions
  be extended to arguments in `Z_3` (3-adic integers) rather than just `N`.
- Averages of Elka functions over the Haar measure on the unit group `Z_3^x` of 3-adic integers,
  a sequence of integral-preserving operators `S_l` on `C(X)` shown to converge to a limiting
  operator `S_infinity`, whose essential part defines a Markov chain on `[0,1]` with an invariant
  density `phi`.
- Three new conjectures (Conjecture 1, 2, 3, none named "covering" and none stated in terms of
  `R_{j,k}`) about growth-rate conditions on `phi` and related quantities, explicitly flagged in
  the abstract as "three unbridged gaps in the argument," needed to complete the positive-density
  proof via this route.

No table of numeric values (of `j*(l)`, `e_l(k,a)`, or anything else resembling this project's
verified l=1..23 table) appears anywhere in the paper. This is a purely analytic/ergodic-theory
argument, not a computational one.

## Relevance to this project

Low-to-none for H-001's direct computational extension (no covering data, no R_{j,k} discussion)
and low for H-002 (the WCC=>beta=1 bridge is specific to Chapter V's framework, untouched here).
The one thing worth registering: Wirsching himself apparently pursued predecessor density via a
**parallel, independent route** that bypasses the covering conjecture entirely. Whether
Conjectures 1-3 here were ever resolved (by Wirsching or anyone else) is unknown, not checked in
this pass (out of scope for a single read focused on the covering question), and would need its
own literature search if pursued. Logged as a new backlog hypothesis (see HYPOTHESES.md) rather
than either investigated further now or discarded silently, per this project's Rule 8e.
