# L-022: G. J. Wirsching, "Über das 3n+1 Problem"

Elemente der Mathematik 55 (2000), 142-155. Obtained via the researcher's institutional access
(2026-07-28) and read in full (14pp). German-language survey.

## Content

An accessible survey covering: the Collatz graph and Collatz's own heuristic origin story;
Berg-Meinardus's functional-equation reformulation (Satz 2.1); the "T-cycle" reformulation of
the Nur-endlich-viele-Zykel-Vermutung and Steiner's 1978 uniqueness proof for the trivial cycle
(via the Diophantine equation `(2^{k+l}-3^k)h = 2^l-1`); Terras's theorem on the equidistribution
of `T0`/`T1` step-sequences among residues mod `2^n` (Satz 3.1) and Korec's PAD/SPAD asymptotic
predecessor-density results (Satz 3.2/3.3, Satz 4.1 = Applegate-Lagarias 1995).

Section 4 ("Der Rückwärtsprozeß") derives a "Vorgängerformel" (predecessor formula) for
`b` given a Collatz-graph path of `k` T0-steps and `l` T1-steps to a target `a`, in terms of the
edge-positions `alpha_0 > ... > alpha_{l-1}` of the T1-steps. This is the same object as this
project's `R_{j,k}` (`Rest = sum_j 2^{alpha_j} 3^j`, matching under `l=j+1`), and the paper's
"Satz 4.2" (attributed to the 1998 book) is the same fact as `notes/H-004.md`'s already-verified
`R_{J-1,J}=R(F_{J,J})` identity, restated informally: each back-tracing tuple determines a unique
admissibility residue class mod `3^l`, not a covering/surjectivity statement. Full derivation and
numerical check in `notes/H-003.md` (2026-07-28 dated section, "Wirsching 2000 survey's 'Satz
4.2'"). Does not bear on j*(l) beyond what H-004 already established.
