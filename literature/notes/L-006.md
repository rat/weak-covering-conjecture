# L-006: T. Tao, "Almost all orbits of the Collatz map attain almost bounded values"

arXiv:1909.03562 (math.NT), first submitted 2019-09-08, latest revision v7 2026-07-16.
Published: Forum Math. Pi 10 (2022), Paper No. e12, 56pp. This is the peer-reviewed, published
paper corresponding to the blog post already reviewed as L-003; fetched and grepped in full
text (via `pdftotext -layout` on the arXiv PDF) rather than relying on the blog post's informal
writeup, per Rule 11 (primary source over secondary summary).

## Abstract (verbatim)

"Define the Collatz map Col : N+1 -> N+1 ... The infamous Collatz conjecture asserts that
Col_min(N)=1 for all N in N+1. Previously, it was shown by Korec that for any theta >
log 3/log 4 ~ 0.7924, one has Col_min(N) <= N^theta for almost all N in N+1 (in the sense of
natural density). In this paper we show that for any function f : N+1 -> R with
lim_{N -> infinity} f(N) = +infinity, one has Col_min(N) <= f(N) for almost all N in N+1 (in the
sense of logarithmic density)."

## Direct confirmation: Wirsching is cited by Tao

Reference [24] in the published paper's bibliography is exactly this project's L-001:
"G. Wirsching, The Dynamical System Generated by the 3n+1 Function, Lecture Notes in Math.
No. 1681, Springer-Verlag: Berlin 1998." This had already been independently re-derived (see
L-003's note, "object-identity check") without relying on Tao citing Wirsching explicitly; this
is now directly confirmed from the primary source itself, not just inferred.

The citation appears once in the body, footnote 5 (Section 1.2, right after Proposition 1.9's
statement about the "3-adic Markov process" behavior of the Syracuse valuation): "This Markov
process may possibly be related to the 3-adic Markov process for the inverse Collatz map studied
in [24]. See also a recent investigation of 3-adic irregularities of the Collatz iteration in
[23]." Two things worth being precise about:

1. Tao's own framing is explicitly speculative ("may possibly be related"), not a proven or even
   asserted equivalence. Tao does not himself formalize any WCC <=> beta=1 bridge in this paper;
   that bridge is this project's own prior work (L-004, Section 9.1), not something inherited
   from Tao's paper. Useful for H-002: the entropy-count bridge really is this project's own
   contribution to formalize, not something already done by Tao that just needs reverification.
2. [23] is a distinct, separate paper: A. Thomas, "A non-uniform distribution property of most
   orbits, in case the 3x+1 conjecture is true," Acta Arith. 178 (2017), no. 2, 125-134, on
   arXiv as 1512.05852 (now L-008 in this index). Worth checking directly for H-002/H-004, see
   below.

## Syracuse random variable machinery, checked against the blog post's claims

n-Syracuse valuation (Section 1.2, around eq. 1.19-1.20), n-Syracuse offset map F_n, and the

construction; a deep line-by-line reconciliation of every symbol was not done (out of scope for
this pass), but no contradiction was found between the two for the pieces relevant to H-002.
Did not locate an explicit named constant "beta" or infimum-probability quantity `c_n` in the
portions of the text grepped (Proposition 1.9, Proposition 1.11, Proposition 1.14, Remark 1.15);
the equidistribution claims here are framed via total-variation-distance decay bounds
(`d_TV(...) << 2^{-c_1 n}`) rather than an explicitly named infimum constant. This does not
contradict L-003 (the blog post may state the same content with different notation, or `beta`
may appear later in the paper, past what was grepped); flagged as unresolved, not as a
discrepancy, since it wasn't chased to full resolution in this pass.
