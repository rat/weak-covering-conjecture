# H-002: Independently reverify the WCC => beta=1 bridge (Proposition beta-wcc)

Status: backlog. Opened 2026-07-22.

## Precise scope (narrowed 2026-07-22, see below)

The previous paper's Proposition (`prop:beta-wcc`, "Structural identity", main.tex l.1370-1381)
makes two separate claims. Do not conflate them; they need different verification:

1. **Object identity**: after a unit twist, Tao's Syracuse random variable and Wirsching's
   R_{j,k} sums are the same algebraic object. **Already independently re-derived and confirmed**
   (2026-07-22, see literature/notes/L-003.md for the full derivation): reindexing Tao's sum and
   multiplying by the unit `2^{b_{n-1}}` turns the increasing, negative-exponent-of-2 form into
   Wirsching's decreasing, non-negative-exponent-of-2 form exactly. This part of the Proposition
   checks out and does not need further work.

2. **The entropy-count bridge**: WCC (Conjecture 3.9, sub-exponential K(l)) implies beta=1, via
   "an exact entropy count: j = log_4(3)*l + o(l) gives c_l >~ 3^{-l(1+o(l))}". This converts a
   SET-COVERING statement (every residue hit by at least one element of R_{j,k}) into a
   PROBABILITY LOWER BOUND (every residue has probability >= 3^{-l+o(l)} under the specific
   non-uniform measure from Tao's geometric weights). This conversion is the one non-mechanical
   step and **has not yet been independently re-derived**; it is what this hypothesis should
   actually verify.

The paper is explicit, not sloppy, about the converse: "beta=1 alone gives only an exponentially
weakened form of Conjecture 3.9 back" - there is no claimed bidirectional equivalence to check.
Framing this hypothesis as "verify the equivalence" would be imprecise; frame it as "verify the
one-directional bridge, and confirm the paper's own statement of the (weak) converse is right."

## Why this matters

The whole project's motivation (extending j*(l), reading e(l)'s growth as evidence for/against
WCC) is only evidence for Tao's beta=1 conjecture, and by extension for Collatz preimage
density, to the extent this bridge holds. If the bridge has a gap, the computational work in
H-001 remains valid as a direct test of Wirsching's own conjecture, but the researcher's stated
broader framing ("this project... contributes to one of the most important steps toward the
full [Collatz] conjecture") would need softening for the eventual paper (Rule 10b: label the
result by what it actually is).

## How to verify

Re-derive the entropy count directly: given a family R_{j-1,j} covering Z_3^* mod 3^l with
j = log_4(3)*l + o(l), derive the implied lower bound on c_l = inf_b P(Syrac(Z/3^l Z) = b) under
Tao's specific geometric-weight measure, not just under uniform counting measure on R_{j-1,j}'s
image. The subtlety: covering says only that some geometric-digit representation of each residue
exists with an EXPONENT SEQUENCE realizable within R_{j-1,j}'s indexing set; the probability mass
of the SPECIFIC representation matters for the bound on c_l, since Tao's measure is not uniform
over sequences. Check whether the "exact entropy count" claimed in the paper handles this
correctly, or only bounds the counting measure and calls it a probability bound informally.

Escalation: this is exactly the kind of subtle, non-mechanical mathematical judgment call
Rule 11b calls for a second opinion on (stronger or independent model, or the researcher) before
either confirming or refuting it, rather than self-certifying. Not yet done as of 2026-07-22;
next step when this hypothesis moves to in-progress.
