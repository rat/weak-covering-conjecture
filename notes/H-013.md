### The mod-9 shadow of Theorem 1, and what it proves

Write units mod 9 as powers of 2 (2 is a primitive root mod 9: classes
`1,2,4,8,7,5 = 2^0..2^5`), and let `E(l,j) subset Z/6` be the set of discrete
logs of the mod-9 classes met by `H(l,j)`. Theorem 1
(`H(l,j+1) subset 2H(l,j) intersect 4H(l,j)`, valid for `j>=l`) projects to

```text
E(j+1) subset sigma(E(j)),   sigma(E) := (E+1) intersect (E+2)  in Z/6,
```

and iterates to `E(j+t) subset intersection_{s=t..2t} (E(j)+s)`. Three
consequences, all proved (the first two also hold verbatim at any modulus `3^m`
with `Z/6` replaced by `Z/(2*3^(m-1))`):

1. **Mod-9 run bound.** If `E(l,j)` is a proper subset of `Z/6` with longest
   cyclic run of consecutive elements `r`, then `j*(l) <= j + r`. (A holdout
   surviving `t` more budgets forces a run of length `t+1`.) This is weaker
   than the round-4 bootstrap but computable from a class histogram alone.
2. **Forcing criterion.** If the iterated shadow image of `E(j*-1-t)` after
   `t` steps is a singleton, then `H(l,j*-1)` occupies a single class mod 9,
   and the class is the singleton's value. Single-class-ness at the end
   becomes a mechanical consequence of class extinction a few budgets earlier.
3. **Mod-3 dichotomy.** In `Z/2` the same map gives
   `E_3(j+1) subset E_3(j) intersect (E_3(j)+1)`: a holdout set occupying a
   single class mod 3 has empty successor. So every pre-final `H(l,j)` with
   `j>=l` contains both classes mod 3, and only the final set can collapse.
   (The main line's theorem (2) is stronger at the final budget: the last set
   MUST be single-class `1 mod 3`. The dichotomy adds the pre-final mixedness,
   which (2) does not give.)

### Exact per-budget sweep, l=5..21: the forcing actually fires at every level

Computed exactly at every level `l=5..21` and every budget from `l` (from `l+1`
at `l=21`) to `j*-1`. Uniform findings, zero exceptions:

- `E(l,j) = Z/6` (all six classes present) for every `j <= j*(l)-4`, and
  `E(l,j)` is proper for every `j >= j*(l)-3`. Mod-9 class extinction begins
  exactly three budgets before coverage, at every computed level.
- At `j = j*-2` the class set is always `{a, a+1}` or `{a, a+1, a+4}` for some
  `a`, and `sigma` of either shape is the singleton `{a+2}`. So the
  **single-class law at `j*-1` is forced by Theorem 1 from the penultimate
  budget's class content at all 17 computed levels**, with the observed class
  `4^(j*+1) mod 9` (equivalently `2^(a+2)`) matching the forced value every
  time. Forcing from `j*-3` alone succeeds at 9 of 17 levels, exactly those
  whose `E(j*-3)` has four elements; it fails (image size 3) at the eight
  levels with five-element `E(j*-3)`, namely `l=7,8,9,16,17,18,19,20`. From
  `j*-2` it succeeds always.
- The mod-9 run bound is exactly tight (`j* = j + r`) at every proper budget
  except the 5-element `E(j*-3)` cases just listed, where it has slack 2.
- The main line's candidate cylinder lemma (5?) (`all x == 4^(j+1) mod 9
  covered at budget j`, for `j >= l+3`) holds at every computed instance
  through `l=21`; the new instances beyond the earlier `l<=15` verification
  are the final budgets of `l=16..21`.

Caveat, stated plainly: this converts "unexplained observation at `j*-1`" into
"mechanical consequence of the class content at `j*-2`", per level. The class
content at `j*-2` is itself still empirical. Combined with the main line's
two-class theorem (4), the entire residual mystery of the mod-9 law is: why
does the penultimate set contain exactly one odd-dlog class (equivalently, why
is `4^J` excluded at the last budget)? Nothing here proves that uniformly in `l`.

### Ancestry is exact: the minority classes ARE the final family

Let `b = j*(l)-1`, `e_f` the final class's dlog. Checked as set equalities on
the exact dumps, `l=16..21`:

```text
2^-s H(l,b) = { x in H(l,b-s) : dlog9(x) == e_f - s (mod 6) },   s=1,2.
```

EQUAL at every level, both depths (for `s=1` this refines the main line's
theorem (3); the `s=2` statement is new). The `l=16, s=2` case was outside the
dump-analysis run (no `(16,17)` dump) and was verified separately by direct DP:
also EQUAL (1182 = 1182). At `s=3` (budget `j*-4 = l` for `l=17..20`, where
only histograms were computed) the cardinality version holds: the
class-`(e_f-3)` count at budget `l` equals `|H(l,b)|` exactly
(1110, 770, 430, 183 at `l=17,18,19,20`; `l=21`'s `s=3` budget was not
computed).
So three budgets before extinction, an entire mod-9 class of the holdout set
consists of nothing but the (halved) future final holdouts. The doomed family
is "reserved" that early.

Related run-structure data (same dumps): among the `l=16..21` dumped budgets
the ends of the maximal doubling runs lie in a single mod-9 class, with exactly
one exception: `H(17,18)` has 1111 maximal 3-runs, 1110 ending in class 1 (the
future final family) and one ending in class 7, whose family dies before
`j*-1`. (At `l<=16` the Python sweep shows two-class maximal-run ends do occur
at a few early budgets, e.g. `l=5 j=5`, `l=7 j=8`, `l=8 j=9`, `l=9 j=10`,
`l=12 j=13`; single-class ends are the rule only near extinction.) Bootstrap tightness at these levels is carried by the surviving
1110; an individual maximal run does not guarantee survival (consistent with
the main line's `l=7, y=1547` reverse-inclusion counterexample).

### Cross-level inheritance travels only the x4 branch (now a theorem)

Round 4 measured the x4-drift descendance `H_(l+1) -> 4 H_l` without asking
about the x2 branch. Exact 2x2 decomposition at the transitions
`16->17,...,20->21` (all `Delta j*=1`): for `y` in the new level's last set,
classify its two Theorem-1 ancestors `y/2, y/4` (both necessarily in
`H(l+1, b)`) by whether their reductions mod `3^l` lie in `H(l, b)`:

| transition | via-4 lift (inherited) | via-2 lift | neither (fresh) |
|:---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16->17 | 359 | 0 | 751 |
| 17->18 | 223 | 0 | 547 |
| 18->19 | 106 | 0 | 324 |
| 19->20 | 29  | 0 | 154 |
| 20->21 | 11  | 0 | 32  |

The via-2 column being zero is not luck; it is a corollary of the main line's
theorem (2): last holdouts at level `l` are `== 1 mod 3`, so their lifts are
`== 1 mod 3`, while `y == 1 mod 3` forces `y/2 == 2 mod 3`. **Cross-level
inheritance of last holdouts can only use the x4 map.** This upgrades the
round-4 "x4 drift" from an observed pattern to the only arithmetically
possible channel.

The fresh majority splits over the mod-729 families without concentrating:
at `19->20`, family A (`4^(b-13)`) has 13 inherited / 68 fresh, family B 3/19,
other 13/67; the fresh fraction rises from 68% to 74% across the five
transitions as the family dies out. No clean predictor of freshness inside a
fixed mod-243 cylinder was found this round; that remains open.

## The two apparent families are one depth-5 family

Write `b_l=j*(l)-1` for the holdout budget. The dominant class seen in round 3
has the cleaner budget-indexed form

```text
A_b = 4^(b-13) mod 3^6.
```

This gives `4,16,64,256,295` for `l=10..14`, skips as an occupied class at
`l=15`, returns as `451` at `l=16`, then continues as
`346,655,433,274` through `l=17..20`. The apparent one-level lag at `l=16`
was caused by the plateau `b_15=b_16=19`. It is a per-budget drift, not
strictly a per-level drift.

The proposed second family is

```text
B_b = 13 * 4^(b-17) mod 3^6.
```

It is not independent from `A_b`. Since

```text
13 = 4^4 - 3^5,
```

we have `B_b = A_b-3^5 mod 3^6`, and therefore

```text
A_b = B_b mod 3^5.
```

So `4=(11)_3` and `13=(111)_3` seed two different sixth-digit lifts of one
five-digit cylinder. This explains the previously separate-looking families.

## What fails at full precision

The natural strong recurrence does not hold for complete residues. Count a
member `y` of `H_(l+1)` as a drift descendant if

```text
y mod 3^l is in {4x mod 3^l : x in H_l}.
```

The exact counts are:

| transition | drift descendants | all next holdouts |
|:---:|---:|---:|
| 16 to 17 | 359 | 1110 |
| 17 to 18 | 223 | 770 |
| 18 to 19 | 106 | 430 |
| 19 to 20 | 29 | 183 |

Thus the full-set child-lift recurrence is decisively false. The local support
version is much stronger: modulo `3^5`, every next holdout lies in the drifted
previous support in all four transitions. Modulo `3^6`, this holds for all next
holdouts except one member of `H_19` (429 of 430 pass at `18 to 19`); the other
three transitions pass completely at that depth.

## Cross-link to H-012 and verdict

The two residues that falsify H-012's global `Delta j=1` induction at
`(l,j)=(10,14)` are exactly all of `H_10`: `37912` and `47389`. Both end in
`000011_3`, which is `4 mod 3^6`, and they seed the dominant `A_b` cylinder
above. H-012's first failure is therefore not unrelated noise. It is the first
small member of the H-013 obstruction family.

This round gives a real positive local result and a real negative full-precision
result. The `x4` observation survives and becomes cleaner when indexed by the
budget and reduced modulo `3^5`; the `4` and `13` families collapse to one
cylinder, and the complete depth-5 support stabilizes up to multiplication by
4 for `l=17..20`. It does not give a closed form for the exact sets `H_l`, and
it does not determine the asymptotics of `j*(l)`: most exact holdouts are not
lifts of the previous full residues. A proof would still need a structural
reason that the 12-state depth-5 support is invariant and a rule controlling
the higher digits inside those cylinders.
