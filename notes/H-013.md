## The two apparent families are one depth-5 family

Write `b_l=j*(l)-1` for the holdout budget. The dominant class seen in round 3
has the cleaner budget-indexed form

```text
A_b = 4^(b-13) mod 3^6.
```

This gives `4,16,64,256,295` for `l=10..14`, skips as an occupied class at
`l=15`, returns as `451` at `l=16`, then continues as
`346,655,433,274` through `l=17..20`. The apparent one-level lag at `l=16`
was caused by the plateau `b_15=b_16=19`. It is a per-budget drift, not
strictly a per-level drift.

The proposed second family is

```text
B_b = 13 * 4^(b-17) mod 3^6.
```

It is not independent from `A_b`. Since

```text
13 = 4^4 - 3^5,
```

we have `B_b = A_b-3^5 mod 3^6`, and therefore

```text
A_b = B_b mod 3^5.
```

So `4=(11)_3` and `13=(111)_3` seed two different sixth-digit lifts of one
five-digit cylinder. This explains the previously separate-looking families.

The common depth-5 cylinder contains every holdout at `l=10..15`. At

There is also a stable coarse support, not just one dominant cylinder. Put

```text
S = {19,28,46,55,73,100,109,127,181,190,208,235} mod 243.
```

For every checked `l=17,18,19,20`, exactly

```text
support(H_l mod 243) = 4^(b_l-19) S.
```

This is an exact equality of the observed support sets. At `l=16`, the support
is `S` plus one extra class, `199`. The twelve elements of `S` in five-digit
base 3 are

```text

11001 11201 20201 21001 21201 22201.
```

This is the cleanest finite-state pattern found in this round. It is data over
four consecutive levels, not a proved invariant.

## What fails at full precision

The natural strong recurrence does not hold for complete residues. Count a
member `y` of `H_(l+1)` as a drift descendant if

```text
y mod 3^l is in {4x mod 3^l : x in H_l}.
```

The exact counts are:

| transition | drift descendants | all next holdouts |
|:---:|---:|---:|
| 16 to 17 | 359 | 1110 |
| 17 to 18 | 223 | 770 |
| 18 to 19 | 106 | 430 |
| 19 to 20 | 29 | 183 |

Thus the full-set child-lift recurrence is decisively false. The local support
version is much stronger: modulo `3^5`, every next holdout lies in the drifted
previous support in all four transitions. Modulo `3^6`, this holds for all next
holdouts except one member of `H_19` (429 of 430 pass at `18 to 19`); the other
three transitions pass completely at that depth.

## Cross-link to H-012 and verdict

The two residues that falsify H-012's global `Delta j=1` induction at
`(l,j)=(10,14)` are exactly all of `H_10`: `37912` and `47389`. Both end in
`000011_3`, which is `4 mod 3^6`, and they seed the dominant `A_b` cylinder
above. H-012's first failure is therefore not unrelated noise. It is the first
small member of the H-013 obstruction family.

This round gives a real positive local result and a real negative full-precision
result. The `x4` observation survives and becomes cleaner when indexed by the
budget and reduced modulo `3^5`; the `4` and `13` families collapse to one
cylinder, and the complete depth-5 support stabilizes up to multiplication by
4 for `l=17..20`. It does not give a closed form for the exact sets `H_l`, and
it does not determine the asymptotics of `j*(l)`: most exact holdouts are not
lifts of the previous full residues. A proof would still need a structural
reason that the 12-state depth-5 support is invariant and a rule controlling
the higher digits inside those cylinders.
